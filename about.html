About HealthPulse

HealthPulse is a free set of calculators for BMI, daily calories, macros, body fat, ideal weight, water, pace and heart-rate zones.

Your numbers never leave your device

Each calculator keeps the unit system and the last figures on this browser only, so the page can reopen where you left it. Clear the site data for HealthPulse and those figures go with it.

Our mission

Make the usual health and fitness numbers easy to check, with the formula written on the page. The arithmetic runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and you do not need an account.

The suite

HealthPulse is the sister site of NetPulse and UnitPulse. The three sites share one design so they read as one suite.

What the numbers are

HealthPulse gives estimates for general information only. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise, especially with a health condition.
