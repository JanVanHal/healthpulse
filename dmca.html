DMCA & Copyright Policy

HealthPulse respects the intellectual property of others and expects the same from anyone who uses the site. This page explains how to report material you believe infringes a copyright, and how we respond.

Last updated: October 2026

Reporting infringement

If you believe content on HealthPulse infringes a copyright you own or are authorised to act for, send a written notice through the contact page. To be effective, include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or a person authorised to act for them.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list if several works are covered.
  • The URL of the material, or enough detail for us to find it.
  • Your name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

What happens next

We review complete notices and, where appropriate, remove or disable the material. We may forward the notice, including your contact details, to the person who provided the material. An incomplete notice may not be acted on.

Counter-notice

If material you provided was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you may send a counter-notice through the contact page. Include your signature, identification of the material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, and your name, address and telephone number, together with consent to jurisdiction as the statute describes.

We may forward a valid counter-notice to the original sender. Unless they tell us they have filed a court action, we may restore the material.

Repeat infringement

We may restrict access for people who repeatedly infringe copyright.

Misrepresentation

Knowingly sending a false notice or counter-notice can create liability. This page is not legal advice.

Contact

Send copyright notices to contact@healthpulse.example or through the contact page, with “DMCA” in the subject.
