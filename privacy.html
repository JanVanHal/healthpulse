Privacy Policy

Your numbers never leave your device. This page says what that means.

Last updated: October 2026

No account

HealthPulse does not ask you to register, and it does not keep a profile of you on a server. The calculators do not send height, weight, age or any other figure you type.

What this browser stores

The metric or imperial choice, and the last figures for each calculator, are stored in this browser only (localStorage) so the page can reopen where you left it. Clearing site data for this site removes them. HealthPulse has no account database to delete.

Fonts

Pages ask your browser to load Inter and Source Serif 4 from Google Fonts. That request can reveal your IP address to Google. It does not include the numbers you typed into a calculator. If the font request fails, the page still works with the fallback fonts.

Advertising

Pages include slots for Google AdSense. The publisher ID on this site is the placeholder ca-pub-1234567890 until a real ID is added, and the ad script is not loaded. If ads are turned on later, Google may use cookies as described in its own policy. You can review ad personalisation at Google ad settings.
