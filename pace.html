How it's calculated

Speed is distance divided by time. Ten kilometres in 50 minutes is 10 ÷ (50 ÷ 60) = 12.0 km/h. Pace in minutes per kilometre is 60 ÷ km/h, so 12.0 km/h is 5:00 per km.

A mile is 1.609344 km, so minutes per mile are minutes per kilometre times 1.609344. 5:00 per km is 8.05 minutes per mile, which is 8:03. Miles per hour are km/h divided by 1.609344, so 12.0 km/h is 7.5 mph.

The same arithmetic is used for running and cycling. The effort is not the same. Presets set the distance to 5 km, 10 km, a half marathon (21.0975 km) or a marathon (42.195 km). The split table walks that distance one unit at a time, or every five units if the distance is longer than 60.

FAQ

Which two numbers do I need?

Any two of distance, time, and pace or speed. Leave the third blank and it is filled in.

What is 10 km in 50:00?

5:00 per kilometre, 12.0 km/h, and 8:03 per mile.

Are cycling and running the same math?

The pace math is the same. The effort is not. A 30 km/h bike ride and a 12 km/h run are different days.

What distances are the presets?

5 km, 10 km, a half marathon at 21.0975 km, and a marathon at 42.195 km. The unit toggle shows those distances in miles when imperial is on.
