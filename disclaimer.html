Health Disclaimer

HealthPulse gives estimates for general information only. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise, especially with a health condition.

Last updated: October 2026

Estimates, not a check-up

BMI, calories, macros, body fat, ideal weight, water and heart-rate zones come from published equations. They describe groups of adults. They do not measure you. A tape, a scale and an equation can all be wrong for a person.

Who the formulas were built for

The equations on this site are adult formulas. They are a poor guide for children, during pregnancy, and for people with an eating disorder or a heart, kidney or metabolic condition. If that is you, talk to a doctor before you change how you eat or train.

Training and diet

A calorie target, a macro split or a heart-rate zone is a planning figure. It is not a promise of weight change, and it is not a reason to ignore pain, dizziness, or a clinician who already knows your history.
